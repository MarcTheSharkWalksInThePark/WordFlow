## 3. Harness integrity

### Judgments

**(a) One recapture for word-range PNGs. Judgment: cannot hide a real product difference.**

- A recapture is allowed only after the font, render style, classes, `outerHTML`, word rectangle
  and text rectangle are rechecked strictly on both pages.
- The second pair must then be byte-equal, with no tolerance.
- Rendering of an unchanged DOM is deterministic. A real difference in how the word is painted
  would show in the second capture too, or in the strict DOM, font and geometry checks.
- The rule could only hide a difference that appears in some captures and not others with
  identical DOM. The product has no animation or transition on the word.
- In practice it was never used. **Recaptures: 0** in Codex's committed run and **0** in my
  parity run.
- With production CSS (no flat backdrop), my own probe needed a recapture in 7 of 618
  master-visible cases. Every first diff was spread across the frame band, at most 1–2 RGB
  levels, over 1306, 3700 or 9900 pixels. Every second capture was exactly equal. That is the
  same capture effect Codex disclosed. Its source is **not verified** by me either.

**(b) Normalising the empty `style=""` in the restart comparison only. Judgment: cannot hide a
rendering difference. It does hide a real, rendering-neutral DOM difference, which Codex's
description understates.**

- Font and rectangle are compared separately and unnormalised.

## 4. R-1 with my own scripts

**Method** (`scripts/r1.cjs`, `runs/r1/`):

- Every case runs on freshly reloaded master and branch pages, in separate browser contexts, so
  both pages have the same history and stay in the foreground.
- **Direct path:** load `Lead <word>` at index 1, then `completeReading()`.
- **Natural path:** the real UI. Paste `We read about <word>` or `Vi leser om <word>`, Load, Use
  source, size button, WPM slider 900, then Play through the countdown and all words. If the
  branch auto-pauses on a scroll-state last word, one Play finishes. `completeReading()` is never
  called.
- Production CSS throughout, and no normalisation anywhere.
- **Paint test:** I capture the frame region (±30 px) with the word painted, and again with it
  hidden. To hide it I use a Web Animation, which leaves the DOM and the `style` attribute alone
  and is allowed under the CSP. Every differing pixel is a pixel the word paints.
  - Outside the inner frame, any such pixel counts as a collision.
  - Inside the panel's painted shape (inset by its border radius) or any control in the frame,
    a change above 16 levels means the word is painted above it. The 96%-opaque panel limits a
    word beneath it to about 4%.
- The pointer is parked and hover transitions are allowed to settle before each capture.
